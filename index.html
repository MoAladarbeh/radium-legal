Radium

Radium runs faceless short-video series for creators. Pick a niche, a voice and a style, and Radium writes each episode, generates the images and voice-over, renders the video with captions and music, and posts it to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts on your schedule.

  1. Write: researched scripts with sources, in your series' style.
  2. Produce: AI images, voice-over, animated captions and music.
  3. Review: edit the storyboard and approve before anything goes out.
  4. Post: uploads to the accounts you connect, labeled as AI-generated.

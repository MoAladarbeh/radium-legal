Radium is a tool that writes, produces and posts short videos to the creator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what personal data Radium processes, why, and what your rights are.

3. Legal basis

We process this data to provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Usage and billing records are kept to meet legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and to prevent abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. YouTube

Radium uses the YouTube API Services. When you connect YouTube, Radium asks for two permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and view your YouTube account (youtube.readonly, used only to read your channel's name, ID and picture). Radium uploads only the videos you create in Radium and marks them as containing AI-generated (synthetic) media.

By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Radium's access at any time on the Google security settings page or by disconnecting the account in Radium.

Radium's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. TikTok

Radium uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. It only posts videos you created in Radium (directly, or to your TikTok drafts) with the privacy and interaction settings you choose, and labels them as AI-generated. You can revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services, or by disconnecting the account in Radium. TikTok's own processing is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Service providers

To run Radium we use the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, login and file storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude): writing scripts from your series settings and topic.
  • Google (Gemini): voice-overs; Google Fonts for the website's fonts.
  • fal.ai or OpenArt: generating images and short animated clips.
  • Google / YouTube and TikTok: when you connect and post to those accounts.

The AI providers receive only the text and images needed to make your video, not your login data or social media tokens. Some of these providers are located in the United States. Transfers there rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Series, scripts and media: until you delete them or your account.
  • Social media tokens: deleted as soon as you disconnect the account.
  • Usage and credit records: as long as required for billing and tax purposes.

To delete your account and all its data, email us from the address you signed up with.

8. Cookies and local storage

Radium does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps you logged in.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, and to object to processing. Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg.

10. Children

Radium is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you by email or in the app.
